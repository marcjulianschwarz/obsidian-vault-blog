Someone stacks pallets into a tower for weeks, then the whole village turns up to watch it burn.

![The pallet stack burning, crowd in the foreground](/photos/kids-and-fire.png)

The interesting part was never the fire. It was the row of heads along the barrier, everyone standing closer than they probably should.

## Stepping back

From the front you only get flames. From the far side of the field you get the actual event: the crowd, the tree line, the smoke pushing sideways, and two firefighters watching it with their hands behind their backs.

![Firefighters watching from the field](/photos/firefighting-action.png)

That frame is the one I keep coming back to. You can feel the heat coming off the left edge, and the rest of the picture is just people standing in a field on a summer evening.

> Film has no live histogram, which for a fire is a small mercy. You guess, you commit, and you find out in two weeks.

Metering was pure guesswork. The fire is many stops above the crowd, so anything that keeps the faces is going to blow out the flames. I exposed for the people and let the fire clip.
