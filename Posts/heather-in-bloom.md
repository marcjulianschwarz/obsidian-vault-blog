The heath flowers for a few weeks in August. Miss the window and it is a brown field. Hit it and the whole ground turns purple-red.

![Wide view over the heath](/photos/lueneburger-heide.png)

The land is flat enough that a small rise gives you everything. Haze stacks the tree lines into bands and the horizon just dissolves.

## Waiting for the sun to drop

Midday is the wrong time here. The heather goes flat and grey-pink, and the sky washes out. An hour before sunset the low light rakes across the plants and the colour comes back.

![Juniper and birch on the ridge](/photos/heather-tree.png)

Two trees on a ridge, nothing else in the frame. Kodak Gold handles the warm end of this well, which is most of why I keep loading it for these trips.

A short list of what actually matters out here:

1. Go in the second half of August
2. Go in the last hour of light
3. Find a rise, however small
4. Wait
