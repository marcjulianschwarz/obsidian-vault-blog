# 'Absolutely spectacular': Artemis II crew see first glimpse of far side of Moon

URL: https://www.bbc.com/news/videos/ce3d5gkd2geo

NASA’s Artemis II crew aboard Orion have, for the first time on this mission, seen and photographed the Moon’s far side — including the Orientale basin.
