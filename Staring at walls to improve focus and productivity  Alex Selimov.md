# Staring at walls to improve focus and productivity | Alex Selimov

URL: https://www.alexselimov.com/posts/men_who_stare_at_walls/

A personal productivity experiment argues that when “brain fog” hits, deliberately **disconnecting from screens** and **staring at a wall for 5–10 minutes (out-of-focus/peripheral vision, aiming for mind-blanking)** can quickly restore focus and motivation by reducing information overload and mental fatigue.
